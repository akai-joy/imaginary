Title: The Wizard's Message
Logline: After surviving a devastating battle, the wounded wizard Alistair uses a magical crow to send a message of survival to his hidden protege, Elynar.

Scene 1 Prompt:
Slow low-angle tracking shot of a wounded wizard, Alistair, in a scorched, torn blue cloak, dragging himself across a smoky, ash-covered battlefield. In the background, a massive, blackened oak tree stands solitary. Embers drift through the air. Overhead, black crows circle in a gloomy, overcast gray sky. Cinematic lighting, dramatic atmosphere, photorealistic, 8k, slow motion.

Scene 2 Prompt:
Medium close-up, slow push-in. Alistair, the weary wizard, leans against the rough, charred bark of the giant oak tree. He extends a trembling hand, casting a soft, pulsing orange magical spell toward a black crow perched on a low branch. The crow's eyes and feathers begin to glow with a faint, warm orange magical aura. Soft cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, particles of light floating, photorealistic.

Scene 3 Prompt:
A panning shot in a sun-drenched, peaceful magic school courtyard. A young girl, Elynar, with wide, hopeful eyes, looks up as a black crow lands gracefully on an ancient stone railing. The crow's eyes briefly flash with a bright orange magical glow, casting a warm light on her face. Bright, ethereal lighting, lush green foliage in the background, cinematic, smooth camera movement.
