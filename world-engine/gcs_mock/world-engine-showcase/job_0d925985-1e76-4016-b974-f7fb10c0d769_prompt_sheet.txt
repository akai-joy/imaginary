Title: The Warmth of Eldoria
Logline: A weary traveler finds comfort and rejuvenation in the peaceful mountain village of Eldoria, from its steaming hot springs to its cozy tavern hearth.

Scene 1 Prompt:
Comic book panel, wide shot. A weary traveler in a heavy winter coat and leather backpack walks along a winding, snow-covered mountain path. In the distance, the warm, golden glowing lights of Eldoria Village nestle among majestic snowy peaks. Dusk sky with deep blues and purples. Detailed line art, rich colors, cinematic lighting.

Scene 2 Prompt:
Comic book panel, high-angle establishing shot. Eldoria Village nestled in a snowy mountain valley. Rustic wooden cabins with snow-covered roofs, warm light spilling from windows. Thick white steam billows up from natural mineral hot springs nestled among dark rocks. Crisp night air, starry sky, detailed comic art style.

Scene 3 Prompt:
Comic book panel, medium shot. The traveler soaking peacefully in a natural stone hot spring pool, eyes closed in deep relaxation. Thick, ethereal steam rises around them, catching the soft light. Above, a clear, brilliant starry night sky. Warm, serene atmosphere, detailed ink lines, rich color palette.

Inside, friendly locals chat around wooden tables. Cozy, inviting atmosphere, detailed comic book illustration, dramatic lighting.

Cozy cabins with glowing windows are dusted in white. Peaceful, serene, cool blue night tones contrasted with the warm wooden window frame, detailed comic art.
